## Recorded design rules

- 16:9, 960 × 540 pt; 48 pt horizontal margins, 24 pt gaps, 16 pt interior padding.
- Midnight plaid palette: navy #1B2A49, red #C41230, body #0F172A, secondary #55595F, white backgrounds. Gold #FDB515 is an accent with appropriate contrast.
- Open Sans body; consistent Helvetica/Arial fallback. JetBrains Mono code, Consolas/Menlo fallback. Institutional wordmarks are a separate typography/asset role.
- Named text tokens: Display 44, Title 32, Lead 20, Body 18, Caption 12, Code 16 pt; detailed tokens also define Label/Header 14, Stat 54 and Wordmark 20.
- One main point per slide; left-aligned content, titles no more than two lines. Use native editable text, meaningful diagrams and evidence-based numbers.
- Header, title, content and page number structure; project label CAPSTONE / MEDTECH AI. The top tartan strip uses accent/highlight/surface/iron/accent in 30/10/30/10/20 proportions.
- Up to six short bullets, three normal cards per row, seven process steps and six table rows by four columns. Terminal samples use a dark card, at most six lines, and actual simulated output.
- Pair status colors with words. Provide speaker notes and render every slide for visual QA.

The source contains small internal differences: the detailed tokens add sizes beyond the short summary; a subtle card shadow is defined despite a general no-shadow preference; cover layouts permit navy backgrounds. Prefer explicit named tokens, restrained cards and adequate contrast.
